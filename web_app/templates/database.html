Normalization Details (Unit 3)
BCNF Verified

Flattened multi-valued arrays of subjects and marks into individual tuples. Defined composite primary key: (register_no, subject_code).

Removed partial functional dependencies where non-key attributes depended only on register_no or subject_code. Decomposed into STUDENTS, SUBJECTS, and MARKS.

Removed transitive dependency register_no → dept_code → dept_name by extracting the DEPARTMENTS relation with dept_id as foreign key.

In all 4 relations (departments, students, subjects, marks), every determinant $X$ in $X \rightarrow Y$ is a superkey. Proven Lossless Join and Dependency Preserving.
