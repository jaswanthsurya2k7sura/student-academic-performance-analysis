What to say to DBMS Faculty:
"Respected faculty, our database tier is modeled and normalized to Boyce-Codd Normal Form (BCNF). We eliminated repeating groups in 1NF, removed partial functional dependencies in 2NF, and decoupled transitive dependencies in 3NF by creating independent DEPARTMENTS, STUDENTS, SUBJECTS, and MARKS relations. Every determinant is a candidate key, satisfying BCNF. We have enforced referential integrity with ON UPDATE CASCADE, indexed foreign keys for $O(\log n)$ performance, and automated grade calculations via SQL triggers and views."
